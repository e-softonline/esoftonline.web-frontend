About Us

Who We Are
Company Overview

eSoft Online is a stable, innovative technology leader with over two decades of industry experience, acting as a trusted managed services and managed security services partner. We work to anticipate and shape change — offering intelligent, secure and scalable solutions across IT infrastructure, cybersecurity and cloud transformation.

Founded in 2003, E-Soft Online has grown from a technology solutions provider to a full-service Managed Security Services Provider (MSSP) and Managed Services Provider (MSP) serving enterprises across industries including BFSI, healthcare, manufacturing, hospitality, retail, and technology. Our team of 150+ certified professionals delivers measurable outcomes through a combination of deep technical expertise, proven methodologies, and a client-first approach.

Driven by Innovation

Technology change is constant and we continually expand our capabilities to match it — from traditional IT infrastructure management to advanced hybrid cloud solutions. This includes deploying enterprise cybersecurity tools, running 24/7 managed SOC services, building business applications, and enabling AI-driven innovation.

Visionary Leadership

Our leadership team combines industry expertise, strategic foresight and operational execution across cybersecurity, cloud strategy, IT infrastructure management and emerging technologies such as AI — aiming for measurable client outcomes at every engagement.
